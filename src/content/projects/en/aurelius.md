A quiet app for reading Stoic passages. No streaks, no badges, no notification
begging you to come back — those mechanics work against the thing the text is
for.

Built in **React Native and Expo** and published to iOS and Android. The design
brief was subtraction: everything that could be removed without losing the
reading experience was removed.
