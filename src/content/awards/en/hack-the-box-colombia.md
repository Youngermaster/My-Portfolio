Hack The Box is a platform of deliberately vulnerable machines you are expected
to compromise without a walkthrough. The ranking is cumulative: it rewards
solving a lot of boxes, and it rewards solving them before everyone else has.

Third in Colombia, reached while working as a security analyst and then as a red
teamer — the ranking and the day job fed each other directly, which is most of
why it was worth the hours.
