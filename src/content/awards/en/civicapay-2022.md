The brief was to add value to **CivicaPay**, the payment application the Medellín
Metro already operates. Rewriting it was not on the table, and would have been
the wrong answer anyway.

What we proposed instead was a points ecosystem connecting three parties that
each wanted something the others had: **companies** looking for qualified people,
**students** looking for experience, and **non-profits** looking for capacity.
Students earn points doing work for non-profits; companies buy into the scheme
for access to a pipeline and the tax treatment that comes with supporting it;
non-profits get the work done. The Metro app becomes the ledger everyone already
has installed.

Built in Flutter, Python 3 and Docker over the length of the hackathon. First
place out of more than 50 teams across 16 universities in Medellín.
