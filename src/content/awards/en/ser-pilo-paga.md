**Ser Pilo Paga** was a Colombian government programme that paid the full cost
of university for students who scored in the top band of the national
standardised test and could not otherwise afford it.

It is the reason I studied engineering at EAFIT rather than not studying at all,
and it is the single largest input into everything on the rest of this page.
