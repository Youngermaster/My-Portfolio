**SIMA** — Sistema de Irrigación y Monitoreo Automatizado. A team of five, an
Arduino, a set of soil moisture sensors, and Ubidots for the dashboard.

The system reads soil moisture and waters on demand rather than on a timer,
which is the entire difference between a plant that survives and a plant that
drowns on schedule. Third place among roughly ten computer science teams in
EAFIT's first cycle of 2019.

This is where the hardware side of my work started, and the line from it to the
robotics projects I write in Rust now is fairly straight.
