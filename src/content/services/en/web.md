I build web front ends that survive contact with real traffic: **Next.js** and
**React** for applications, **Astro** for content-driven sites that have no
business shipping a runtime at all.

Performance is treated as a requirement rather than a later pass — measuring
load behaviour, cutting bundle weight, and making the responsive story work on
a phone as well as it does on a wide monitor. On the Duluth Trading Company
redesign that meant reworking the front end to hold up across desktop, tablet
and mobile at once.
