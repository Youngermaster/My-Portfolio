Most of my mobile work runs on **React Native and Expo**, from e-commerce apps
handling real transaction volume to the small utility apps I publish through
Grisú. The same toolchain covers both, which is why a feature that proves itself
in one usually finds its way into the other.

What that looks like in practice: typed components, unit tests in Jest, device
verification through BrowserStack, and a CI pipeline that builds and signs
releases without anyone opening Xcode. Where a project needs a game loop rather
than a screen stack, I reach for **Godot** instead.
