I design interfaces before building them, which mostly means the handover is to
myself — and that is exactly why the specification ends up complete. For
GameConnect I did the UI design first so implementation could follow a settled
spec instead of negotiating with one.

The site you are reading is the same idea applied to a design system: a small
set of tokens, a handful of primitives, and [a public styleguide](/styleguide)
that makes drift visible rather than letting it accumulate.
